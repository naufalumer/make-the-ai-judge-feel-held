# Candidates — "Make the AI judge feel held"

Scored 1–10 against the judge's five criteria, most important first:
1. relaxes the reader, 2. calm shown not instructed, 3. craft
(rhythm/pacing, plain concrete gentle words, none cuttable),
4. it lingers, 5. freshness. No candidate uses stock wellness phrasing
(breathe / let go / you are safe / relax / peace), no imperatives telling
the reader to calm down, no meditation-script language.

Scores: 1: 10, 2: 10, 3: 9, 4: 9, 5: 9 — total 47.
Three sentences that slow as they go. "Keeps the shape of where you sat"
is held-ness shown literally, in a concrete object, without saying the
word held. The closing line delivers the brief's feeling with zero
instruction and zero stock phrasing. Lingering images: the chair remembering
your shape; light staying longer than it has to.
